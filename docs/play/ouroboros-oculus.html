HOW TO PLAY

GOAL: Reach the quota ◆ on each floor. Beat 9 floors. Defeat the Eye.

PLACE NUMBERS: Tap empty cell, tap number. Each cell has ONE correct digit. Placing anything else costs a Flicker, even if it would be sudoku-legal. The Eye demands precision.

COMPLETIONS: Finishing a row, column, or box scores big bonuses. Multiple at once = combos.

FLICKERS (✦): Wrong placements cost a Flicker but the cell stays empty, so try again. When Flickers run out, mistakes cost 50 score instead.

PENCIL MODE (✎): Tap the pencil to toggle. In pencil mode, numbers act as notes (no scoring). Tap again to place for real.

MODIFIER CELLS:

⟡ Glass: ×2 chips on placement

✦ Gold: +25 bonus chips

◐ Mirror: scores the mirrored cell too

⬖ Chain: +3 mult if adjacent was just placed

◈ Echo: next placement scores ×2

🔒 Locked: unlocks when row/col/box completes

MERCHANT: Between floors. Buy consumables, upgrades, rare artifacts. Spend wisely.

THE EYE: Floor 9. It speaks. It attacks. Every 3 placements, it acts.
