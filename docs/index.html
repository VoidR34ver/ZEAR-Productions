Hadal

Playtest build

You are a lab animal with a "hypergene" that lets you evolve every time you level up. You're set loose in a procedurally generated ocean trench whose ecosystem lives and evolves on its own, from sunlit reef shelf down to the hadal deep.

  • Five depth zones with sub-biomes, from reef shelf to hadal trench
  • 98 starter species, from 7 cm reef fish to a 20 m cave predator
  • Creature bodies and skin grown from a genome, so species keep changing
  • Combat with stamina, a burst dodge, charged bites and enemy tells
  • Pick one of three mutations at each level-up, five starting classes
  • Weather, storm-driven waves and currents, and you can leave the water

One House Away From Home

Early playtest

First-person gothic survival horror: what if RE4 and RE8 had a baby in RE1. Graubünden, 1998: a castle built through a mountain, each area in its own architectural style. Every bullet is both your weapon and a key.

  • One castle of 15 buildings and 228 rooms, all reachable
  • Two guns, and the ammo also opens shootable locks
  • Named keys, ladders and a Resident-Evil-style map
  • Zombies that hear the noise you make
  • Ray-traced echoes and room-sized reverb

Honest status: placeholder HUD, no terrain around the castle yet, some doors lead nowhere, and enemies and story are thin.

Playtest

Paste one line into a terminal. It downloads the newest build and starts it. Run it again to update.

Linux (x86_64, Vulkan, needs curl and unzip)

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoidR34ver/ZEAR-Productions/OHAfH/play.sh | sh

Windows 10/11 (64-bit, low-spec build, paste into PowerShell)

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoidR34ver/ZEAR-Productions/OHAfH/play.ps1 | iex

No macOS build yet. Or download the zips directly from the ohafh-latest release.

Keys: WASD move, mouse look, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, E interact, 1 / 2 weapons, F flashlight, left click fire, right click aim, M map, Esc pause.
