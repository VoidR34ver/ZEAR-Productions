hadal

deep-ocean evolution game · Windows, macOS, Linux · playtest build

You are a lab animal with a "hypergene". Every time you level up you pick one of three mutations, and your body changes. Then you go back into the trench and try not to get eaten.

The trench is generated, and the ecosystem in it runs and evolves on its own whether you're watching or not. There are 98 starter species, from 7 cm reef fish to a 20 m cave predator, and none of them keep the same body for long. Their shapes and skin grow out of a genome.

It goes from the reef shelf down through five depth zones to the hadal floor. Fights run on stamina: dodge, charge a bite, watch for the tell before something lunges. Storms push waves and currents around up top, and you can leave the water if you want to.

One House Away From Home

first-person survival horror · Graubünden, 1998 · early playtest

What if RE4 and RE8 had a baby in RE1. A castle built through a mountain, fifteen buildings and 228 rooms, every wing in a different architectural style and lit by its own colour of lamp. All of it rendered properly and then crushed through a low-res PSX dither.

You get two guns. The ammo is also how you get through shootable locks, so every bullet you spend on a zombie is a door you might not open later. There are named keys, ladders and a proper Resident Evil map. Zombies hear what you do. Rooms echo the way rooms that size should.

It's early. The HUD is a placeholder, there's no terrain around the castle yet, some doors go nowhere, and there isn't much enemy or story content.
