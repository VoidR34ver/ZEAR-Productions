Hadal

  • Deep-ocean evolution game
  • Windows, macOS, Linux
  • Playtest build

You are a lab animal with a "hypergene". Every level-up you choose a mutation, and your body changes.

The trench is generated, and the ecosystem in it runs and evolves on its own whether you're watching or not. None of the species keep the same body for long: their shapes and skin grow out of a genome, so what hunted you last time might look different now.

Download the latest build

Pick the zip for your system, unzip it, run it. It updates itself to newer builds.

  1. Five depth zones, from the reef shelf down to the hadal trench.
  2. 98 starter species, from 7 cm reef fish to a 20 m cave predator.
  3. Three mutation offers at every level-up, five starting classes.
  4. Fights on stamina: a burst dodge, charged bites, and tells before something lunges.
  5. Weather, storm-driven waves and currents.
  6. You can leave the water.
02

One House Away From Home

First-person survival horror · Graubünden, 1998 · Early playtest

What if RE4 and RE8 had a baby in RE1. A castle built through a mountain: fifteen buildings and 228 rooms, every wing in a different architectural style and lit by its own colour of lamp. All of it rendered properly, then crushed through a low-res PSX dither.

You get two guns, named keys, ladders and a proper Resident Evil map. Zombies hear what you do. Rooms echo the way rooms that size should.

Every bullet you spend on a zombie is a lock you might not shoot open later.

It's early. The HUD is a placeholder, there's no terrain around the castle yet, some doors go nowhere, and there isn't much enemy or story content.

Play it

Paste one line into a terminal. It downloads the newest build and starts it. Run it again to update.

Linux · x86_64, Vulkan, needs curl and unzip
curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoidR34ver/ZEAR-Productions/OHAfH/play.sh | sh
Windows 10/11 · PowerShell, low-spec build
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoidR34ver/ZEAR-Productions/OHAfH/play.ps1 | iex

No Mac build yet. The zips are also on the release page.

WASD move, mouse look, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, E interact, 1/2 weapons, F flashlight, left click fire, right click aim, M map, Esc pause.
